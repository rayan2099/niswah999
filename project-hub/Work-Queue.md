# Work queue and decisions

Open [the dashboard](Work-Queue.base) in Obsidian for sortable/filterable authored work items and decisions. These statuses are manually maintained and are independent of evidence/publication gates. No current project tasks are invented or automatically marked complete.

Create notes in `project-hub/work-items/` or `project-hub/decisions/` with [the templates](templates/Work-Item.md). Add Markdown links to source records and generated feature maps. Obsidian backlinks expose their connections.

Existing tasks remain in [master findings](../production-readiness-results/master/00_04_MASTER_FINDING_REGISTER.md), [owner checklist](../docs/final-owner-launch-checklist.md), and [GitHub issues](https://github.com/rayan2099/niswah999/issues); inspect their dates and current evidence before adopting a status.
