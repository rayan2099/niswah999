## Decision and rationale

1. **Missed-period threshold (prediction, not fiqh).** `predictFromIntervals` leaves out an interval that is at least **1.8×** the median of the other qualifying intervals (`CycleCalculationResult.missedPeriodMedianFactor`), down from 2.0×. A lone interval is always kept, and the rule never drops every interval. *Reason:* a missed period is two cycles summed, so with normal variation it often lands just under 2× (69 among 35-day cycles). At 2.0× those gaps were still counted and skewed predictions by weeks.
2. **Nifas window: 40 days for every madhhab (pending scholar confirmation).** Day 0 to day 40 after the logged birth date is the window shown for every madhhab. *Reason:* it is the majority position and the shorter maximum, so the app never treats prayer as lifted beyond day 40 on its own authority.
3. **Shafi'i/Maliki bleeding on day 41–60: advisory only (pending scholar confirmation).** These schools allow up to 60 days. For a Shafi'i or Maliki user with an open bleeding episode on day 41–60, the app states **no ruling**: neither "nifas continues" nor "tahara, pray". Today shows a "Please consult a scholar" card (EN/AR). The ring and the prayer card read "needs review", and the Fiqh banner is hidden. The Fiqh report, and with it the Husband and Doctor reports, gives `needsAdvisory` with a dedicated advisory note. The AI advisor's client state is `needsAdvisory`. *Reason:* her school may still count this bleeding as nifas, while the Haid engine would rule on it as ordinary bleeding. *Smallest correct version:* bleeding that continues past day 40 was not modelled. The app does not tell continuous bleeding apart from bleeding that resumed after a clean gap, so any open episode in that range gets the advisory. That is conservative because no ruling is stated either way.
4. **Nifas day numbering: decided convention.** Days are counted 0-based from the logged birth date everywhere. The dashboard card shows "Birth day" / "يوم الولادة" on day 0, then "Day 1/40" through "Day 40/40", and it is gone on day 41.
5. **Nutfah/Alaqah/Mudghah labels (pending scholar confirmation).** The hadith of Ibn Mas'ud (Sahih al-Bukhari 3208, Sahih Muslim 2643: "forty days… then a clot like that… then a lump like that") is usually read as three 40-day stages counted from conception. Days since conception are taken as gestational age minus 14 days. Nutfah covers days 0–40, Alaqah 41–80 and Mudghah 81–120. Outside those days there is no classical label, only the trimester. The mapping lives in one place, `PregnancyStatusEngine.classicalStageForGestationalDays`. *Reason:* the old gestational-week mapping had no documented basis and counted from the LMP, not from conception.
6. **Pregnancy loss: no new flow (pending scholar confirmation).** Ending pregnancy tracking without a logged birth (turning "I am currently pregnant" off, which goes through `PregnancyStateSync.deactivate` / `markTrackingEnded`) never starts nifas and states no ruling about the bleeding that follows. The confirmation shows a neutral EN/AR note: whether bleeding after a pregnancy loss counts as nifas depends on how far the pregnancy had developed, so please consult a scholar. *Reason:* the ruling depends on how far the pregnancy had developed, which the app cannot assess. A real loss flow still needs joint scholar and medical review.
7. **FIQH-7 purity gate (pending scholar confirmation).** The gate applies to all madhahib and measures purity from the last possible haid. It is kept as the conservative default. No code changed.

## Impact and verification

- Tests: `test/round2_properties/` (P7, P10 widened to the 1.8× rule; regressions R2-7b/c/d for `[35,35,69]`, `[35,69]` and the guards). `test/pregnancy_notifications_hardening/` contains `nifas_window_decision_test.dart`, `classical_stage_mapping_test.dart`, `pregnancy_tracking_ended_no_nifas_test.dart` and an updated `dashboard_trimester_label_test.dart`.
- Generated irregular histories (20,000 seeds of the property generator's `irregular` kind, genuine cycles only, no missed logs) were checked with the leave-one-out rule. At 1.8× it drops 1,073 of 99,709 intervals (1.08%), against 477 (0.48%) at 2.0×, so 568 histories (2.8%) gain a drop. Most of these come from the generator's floor of 15 days (for example `[44, 33, 15, 23]`). The `regular` and `shortLong` kinds have no drops at either factor. Dropped intervals are left out of the forward prediction only; `cycleLengths` and `averageCycleLength` are never filtered.
- The server mirror (`supabase/functions/_shared/pregnancy_status.ts`) is unchanged. The engine's phase and window are unchanged (40), and the advisory is computed on the client.
- Limitations: the dashboard reads the birth date logged on the device, while the reports read the synced profile, as before. The advisory triggers on an open episode only; a closed run in days 41–60 is ruled on as usual. The chat assistant's server-side prompt and the Doctor/Husband PDF copy show the generic "needs advisory" label rather than the dedicated text.
- No expert approval is claimed. Every fiqh item remains pending scholar confirmation.
