# Review and Publishing Workflow

## Lifecycle
DRAFT
→ SOURCE_VERIFIED
→ DOMAIN_REVIEWED
→ APPROVED
→ PUBLISHED
→ RETIRED

## Roles
- FIQH: qualified Madhhab-aware scholar
- HEALTH: appropriate medical reviewer
- SAFETY: medical safety reviewer
- PRODUCT: Niswah product/engineering owner

## Rules
- Do not overwrite published history.
- An edit creates a new version.
- Retain source, locator, reviewer, and approval date.
- A rejected item remains auditable.
- Production retrieval excludes DRAFT, SOURCE_VERIFIED, DOMAIN_REVIEWED, REJECTED, and RETIRED versions unless explicitly configured for internal review.

## Fiqh
No ruling becomes APPROVED without exact evidence locator and qualified scholar decision.

## Health
Draft canonical wording may be source-mapped before review but must remain NOT_APPROVED_NOT_PUBLISHED until medical review.
